# Asset provenance

The homepage hero is the supplied generated `assets/source/hero.png` (1672 × 941). It depicts an approved red sedan, which supersedes the earlier graphite-coupe prompt in the planning brief. It is a demo visual and is not identified as a catalogue model.

`assets/source/family-suv.png` and `assets/source/touring-wagon.png` are supplied generated demo visuals (each 1586 × 992). They are used only as unbranded illustrative catalogue subjects.

The ten non-generated vehicle inputs (`pic1.jpg` through `pic8.jpg`, `car2.png`, and `car3.png`) and `logo-light.png` were present in the source export. Their commercial rights and clearance have not been verified. They must not be represented as commercially cleared, manufacturer-authorized, or evidence of a real listing.

Responsive WebP files are mechanical derivatives only: they preserve source aspect ratio and use `withoutEnlargement`. Fonts and icons are copied from the pinned local packages with their upstream license files.
